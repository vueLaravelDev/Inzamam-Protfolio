It depends on scope. A focused module can take a few weeks, and a full system such as a POS or hospital platform is usually planned in milestones over a few months. You get a realistic timeline after the discovery call.

Can you work on my existing Laravel project?

Yes. I can add features, fix bugs, upgrade versions and improve slow queries in an existing Laravel or Vue.js codebase.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. I offer fixes and updates after deployment, either as needed or as an ongoing maintenance arrangement.

How do we communicate during the project?

You get regular progress updates and demos at each milestone. I am reachable by email, phone and WhatsApp, and I reply within 24 hours.
